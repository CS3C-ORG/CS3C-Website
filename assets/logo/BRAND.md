# CS3C Brand Mark

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

---

## Philosophy

The CS3C Mark is a visual representation of the CS3C Knowledge System.

It does not represent cybersecurity. It represents the transformation of
knowledge into reusable engineering assets.

## Meaning

The five horizontal layers represent:

1. Research
2. Methodology
3. Models
4. Workbench
5. Publications

Knowledge flows from broad research toward increasingly structured, reusable
engineering knowledge. Each successive layer becomes shorter, symbolizing
refinement, organization, and abstraction.

## Geometry

The mark consists of five left-aligned horizontal layers.

- All layers have equal height.
- All layers have equal vertical spacing.
- All layers begin at the same left edge.
- Layer widths follow the ratio `5:4:3:2:1`.
- The longest layer is at the top.
- The shortest layer is at the bottom.
- Corners are subtly rounded.

The master mark uses a `40 × 32` viewBox:

| Layer | X | Y | Width | Height | Corner radius |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Research | 0 | 2 | 40 | 4 | 1.5 |
| Methodology | 0 | 8 | 32 | 4 | 1.5 |
| Models | 0 | 14 | 24 | 4 | 1.5 |
| Workbench | 0 | 20 | 16 | 4 | 1.5 |
| Publications | 0 | 26 | 8 | 4 | 1.5 |

These relationships must remain unchanged at every size.

All future logo variants shall derive from this master geometry. The master mark
is the single source of truth for all CS3C visual assets, including the
standalone mark, combined logo, favicon, and future brand materials.

## Colors

The mark uses flat Forest Green tones, progressing from darkest at the top to
lightest at the bottom.

| Layer | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Research | `#14532d` |
| Methodology | `#166534` |
| Models | `#4a8f5b` |
| Workbench | `#7dbd8e` |
| Publications | `#b7dbbf` |

The first two colors match the website's `--accent-strong` and `--accent`
tokens. Do not use gradients, shadows, or outlines.

## Wordmark

The symbol may appear with or without the `CS3C` wordmark.

- The standalone symbol is the **CS3C Mark**.
- The combined symbol and wordmark is the **CS3C Logo**.

The wordmark is not part of the symbol itself. The CS3C Mark and the CS3C Logo
are separate visual assets.

The combined logo uses Inter Semibold and the established CS3C tracking. The
standard wordmark color is `#111827`. The light variant uses `#ffffff` for the
wordmark only; the mark colors never change.

## Correct use

- Preserve the original proportions.
- Preserve left alignment across all five layers.
- Preserve all five layers and their order.
- Maintain generous whitespace.
- Use the supplied SVG assets whenever possible.
- Scale assets proportionally.

## Incorrect use

Do not:

- rotate;
- stretch or compress;
- recolor;
- add effects, gradients, shadows, or outlines;
- change the layer order;
- remove or merge layers;
- center the layers relative to one another.

## Minimum size

- CS3C Mark: 24 px high
- CS3C Logo — Recommended minimum: 120 px wide

Smaller renderings must be tested for readability and must never use a
simplified mark.

## Ownership

The CS3C Brand Mark is part of the CS3C Engineering Knowledge System and should
evolve only through explicit design decisions.
