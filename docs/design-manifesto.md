# CS3C Design Manifesto

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The CS3C design language exists to support understanding.

Our goal is not to impress visitors.

Our goal is to help them understand complex cybersecurity engineering topics through clarity, structure and evidence.

Every design decision should make knowledge easier to consume.

---

# Core Principles

## Evidence over Opinion

Every important statement should be supported by evidence.

Prefer facts over claims.

Prefer methodology over marketing.

---

## Engineering over Marketing

CS3C explains.

CS3C measures.

CS3C documents.

CS3C teaches.

We never exaggerate.

---

## Clarity over Complexity

Cybersecurity is already complicated.

Our interface should reduce cognitive load instead of increasing it.

Simple does not mean simplistic.

---

## Quality over Quantity

Publish fewer things.

Make them exceptional.

Avoid information overload.

---

## Timeless over Trendy

We do not follow short-lived design trends.

Our interfaces should remain modern for years.

---

## Open over Hidden

Whenever possible:

- publish methodology
- explain assumptions
- document limitations
- encourage reuse

Knowledge grows when it is shared.

---

## Precision over Noise

Every sentence should justify its existence.

Every diagram should communicate one idea.

Every visual element should have a purpose.

---

# Design Motto

Content is the interface.

---

# Brand Personality

CS3C is:

- Calm
- Precise
- Engineering-driven
- Open
- Trustworthy

---

# The CS3C Promise

We help people understand cybersecurity engineering through evidence, methodology and practical knowledge.
