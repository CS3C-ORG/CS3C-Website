# CS3C Design Principles

## Purpose

The website should communicate trust, clarity and engineering excellence.

Every design decision should support the content rather than compete with it.

---

# Principles

## 1. Minimal

Remove everything that does not add value.

Whitespace is a feature.

---

## 2. Readable

Long-form reading must be comfortable.

Typography is more important than decoration.

---

## 3. Professional

No unnecessary animations.

No visual noise.

No marketing clichés.

---

## 4. Fast

Pages should load instantly.

Performance is a feature.

---

## 5. Accessible

Readable on desktop and mobile.

Good contrast.

Keyboard-friendly.

---

## 6. Consistent

Spacing.

Typography.

Colors.

Buttons.

Cards.

Everything should feel like one system.

---

## 7. Content First

The content is the product.

The interface exists to present knowledge.
