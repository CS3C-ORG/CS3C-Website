# Design Language

Engineering Minimalism

The interface exists to support content.

Content is always more important than decoration.

# Cards

Simple.

Flat.

White background.

Thin border.

No gradients.

# Images

Preferred

- diagrams
- architecture
- infrastructure
- engineering
- maps
- charts
- real equipment
- laboratories

Avoid

- business stock photos
- people shaking hands
- generic office imagery

---

# Motion

Duration

200–250 ms

Purpose

Motion should explain interaction.

Never distract.

---

# Accessibility

Accessibility is a feature.

Follow WCAG recommendations.

Maintain sufficient contrast.

Support keyboard navigation.

Use semantic HTML.

---

# Tone of Voice

Professional.

Calm.

Evidence-based.

Never use unnecessary marketing language.

---

# Guiding Principle

Content is the interface.
