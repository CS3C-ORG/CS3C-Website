# Homepage Information Architecture

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

---

# Purpose

The homepage is not a marketing landing page.

It is the entry point to the CS3C Engineering Knowledge System.

Its purpose is to help visitors understand:

1. what CS3C is,
2. why it exists,
3. how the knowledge system works,
4. where they should begin.

The homepage defines the platform before inviting visitors to explore it.

Its role is definition, not persuasion.

---

# Homepage Narrative

The homepage follows a simple narrative.

## 1. Identity

"What is CS3C?"

Visitors should immediately understand that CS3C is an independent engineering knowledge system for cybersecurity.

The homepage should avoid marketing language and focus on clear definition.

---

## 2. Purpose

"Why does CS3C exist?"

Cybersecurity knowledge is abundant.

Structured engineering knowledge is not.

CS3C exists to transform research, experience, and evidence into reusable engineering practice.

---

## 3. Knowledge System

"How does CS3C work?"

Visitors should understand that every part of CS3C belongs to one coherent engineering system.

The platform is not a collection of independent products.

It is a connected knowledge ecosystem.

```
Research
      │
      ▼
Methodology
      │
      ▼
Models
      │
      ▼
Workbench
      │
      ▼
Publications
```

Each layer supports the next.

Research produces evidence.

Methodologies transform evidence into repeatable practice.

Models organize engineering knowledge.

Workbench applies those models.

Publications preserve the resulting knowledge.

---

## 4. Exploration

"Where should I begin?"

Only after visitors understand the system should they be invited to explore it.

The homepage should provide clear entry points for different audiences while preserving a single platform narrative.

# Why CS3C Exists

Cybersecurity knowledge is abundant.

Frameworks, standards, reports, tools, and organizational experience continue to grow.

What remains difficult is transforming that knowledge into a coherent engineering system that can be understood, applied, improved, and reused.

CS3C exists to bridge that gap.

---

# The CS3C Knowledge System

The Knowledge System explains how every part of CS3C contributes to practical engineering work.

```
Research

Generates evidence.

↓

Methodology

Transforms evidence into repeatable practice.

↓

Models

Organize engineering knowledge.

↓

Workbench

Applies models in practical work.

↓

Publications

Preserve knowledge for future reuse.
```

This section represents the conceptual architecture of CS3C rather than a navigation menu.

---

# Models

Models organize cybersecurity knowledge into reusable engineering structures.

The Capability Model is the first implementation.

Future models may include:

- Capability Model
- Maturity Model
- Threat Model
- Governance Model
- Dependency Model

---

# Workbench

The Workbench is the practical application layer of CS3C.

It provides engineering tools, templates, structured workflows, and methodologies that help practitioners apply the knowledge system consistently.

Workbench is not presented as a commercial software product.

---

# Research

Research creates the evidence on which the knowledge system is built.

Research should produce:

- observations
- experiments
- analyses
- design decisions
- assumptions
- limitations

Research exists to improve methodologies and models rather than to publish content.

---

# Publications

Publications preserve engineering knowledge.

They include:

- White Papers
- Engineering Notes
- Methodologies
- Reference Documents
- Experiments

Every publication should be traceable to the evidence and reasoning that produced it.

---

# The CS3C Doctrine

The doctrine describes how knowledge is created inside CS3C.

It defines engineering principles rather than organizational values.

Core principles include:

- Evidence before assertion.
- Explicit assumptions.
- Simple before complex.
- Reuse before reinvent.
- Document before automate.
- Traceable decisions.
- Versioned knowledge.
- Clear limitations.

---

# Choose Your Starting Point

Visitors should be guided according to their current work rather than their industry.

Suggested entry points include:

- Leaders
- Security Architects
- Engineers
- Researchers
- Consultants

Each entry point should lead into the same knowledge system through different perspectives.

---

# Writing Principles

Writing throughout CS3C should be:

- precise
- calm
- engineering-first
- evidence-based
- technically literate
- free of marketing language

Every sentence should answer a concrete question.

Confidence should come from clarity and evidence rather than adjectives.

---

# Design Principle

The homepage is not intended to sell the platform.

It is intended to explain the platform.

Understanding always comes before exploration.

---

# Long-Term Vision

CS3C is not simply a website.

It is an Engineering Knowledge System for cybersecurity.

The website is one interface to that system.

Future growth should deepen the knowledge system rather than expand the number of unrelated features.

Every new model, methodology, tool, publication, or research project should strengthen the coherence of the system instead of increasing its complexity.
